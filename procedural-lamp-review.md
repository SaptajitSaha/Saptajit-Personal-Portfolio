# Procedural Desk-Lamp Study — Integration Review

The lazy-loaded scene appears as a distinct full-width procedural-study panel immediately after Selected Work. Its cobalt, coral, warm-metal, and soft studio lighting echo the existing Signal Field atmosphere without duplicating the Nidarr card treatment.

Desktop review confirms a readable scene field with separate title, description, small runtime HUD, contextual selection region, and unobtrusive assembly controls. The model remains the visual focus rather than becoming a competing hero.

On the 390px mobile review, the study retains a contained frame, readable context copy, the full lamp silhouette, and three compact controls arranged along the lower edge. The canvas uses `touch-action: pan-y`, so normal vertical page movement remains available. The scene is deferred until its section approaches the viewport, preventing its Three.js bundle and WebGL work from competing with the opening experience.
