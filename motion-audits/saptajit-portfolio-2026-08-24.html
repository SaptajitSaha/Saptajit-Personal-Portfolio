Overall

The portfolio has a strong authored visual identity and real project evidence, but the current interaction system is undermined by one physical overlap, undersized controls, severely compressed disclosure labels, and too many equally emphatic glass surfaces. The regressions pass; the remaining problems are primarily ergonomic, optical, and maintainability risks that will show up in real use.

Emil Kowalski — Restraint & Speed

Selective

High-frequency navigation and carousel controls should be dependable, comfortably targetable, and effectively invisible in use.

CriticalEmil

Nidarr’s carousel overlaps the “View case study” trigger.

WhatRuntime geometry shows the Nidarr trigger occupying 28px of the phone-carousel stage on desktop and 31px on mobile. Direct pointer automation was intercepted by the carousel until the audit deliberately targeted a different case-study button.

Why it mattersThis is a genuine interaction defect: the visible control may not receive a click or tap at its upper edge. It also explains why the page can feel inconsistent even when keyboard paths and regression tests pass.

Recommended motionReserve a non-overlapping control lane beneath the carousel, or raise the trigger container above the stage with a deliberate stacking boundary. Keep the control static while the carousel moves; do not solve it with more motion.

Evidence: runtime.json · desktop trigger y 2232.7–2268.7 vs carousel y 1704.4–2260.7; mobile y 2486.5–2522.5 vs 2072.1–2517.5 · Home.tsx:190–201

CriticalEmil

Carousel navigation is below comfortable touch-target size.

WhatThe carousel arrows measure 27–29px, autoplay 23–24px, and inactive slide dots 6px. These are materially below common 40–44px touch targets.

Why it mattersSmall, unlabeled visual dots demand precision precisely where users expect a quick exploration control. The reduced target area is amplified by the project card’s complex visual field.

Recommended motionWrap every dot in a transparent 40px button hit area, retain a 6–9px visual dot inside it, and make arrows/autoplay at least 40px. Keep the existing 160–180ms color/scale feedback.

Jakub Krehel — Production Polish

Primary

The overall direction is compelling. The most valuable refinement now is subtraction: clarify typography and hierarchy so the glass system feels intentional rather than repeated.

ImportantJakub

Disclosure labels have extreme negative letter spacing.

WhatMeasured computed letter spacing is −2.20px on desktop and −1.52px on mobile for “View case study”; learning triggers measure −1.15px desktop and −0.93px mobile. At 12–14px text, this compresses wordforms rather than merely tightening them.

Why it mattersThe labels are functional controls, not display type. Compression makes their information scent harder to parse, conflicts with the spacious glass layout, and is especially fragile under browser font substitution.

Recommended motionReset interactive labels to letter-spacing: .01em or normal. Reserve negative tracking for large display headings only. Keep label motion to color/icon rotation rather than text deformation.

ImportantJakub

Every major section uses the same dominant glass-panel treatment.

WhatThe hero, signal strip, work, learning, about, tools, and reach-out regions all present as large bordered, blurred, rounded surfaces. On the long mobile scroll, the page becomes a stack of similarly weighted containers.

Why it mattersGlass is strongest when it establishes hierarchy. Repeating it at the same emphasis makes “section start,” “primary content,” and “supporting information” visually indistinguishable, reducing the impact of the hero and featured Nidarr work.

Recommended motionKeep the hero and reach-out as major glass objects; let at least two middle sections return to the field using typography, a signal line, and lighter dividers. Use card depth for project evidence only.

ImportantJakubEmil

Accordion behavior is stable, but the CSS still contains two competing disclosure systems.

WhatThe new Radix rules animate height with keyframes at lines 201–203, while older clip-path, opacity, filter, and transition rules for the same elements remain at lines 71–82. Old summary/details selectors also remain after the markup moved to Radix triggers.

Why it mattersThis creates a fragile cascade: styles can appear correct today but regress whenever class order, motion preference, or a component wrapper changes. Keyframes are also less interruptible than state-driven transitions for rapid reopens.

Recommended motionDelete legacy disclosure selectors, retain one Radix layout animation, and use one scoped transform/opacity transition on the inner body. Let keyboard actions be instant under reduced motion.

Jhey Tompkins — Experimentation & Delight

Secondary

The portfolio should remain expressive; the adjustment is to spend the visual energy on fewer, more memorable moments.

ImportantJheyEmil

The motion language has too many independent interaction signatures.

WhatButtons lift by 1–2px, press at .96/.975/.98/.985/.992 scales, project cards tilt through pointer-driven custom properties, the carousel shifts in 440ms, accordions use 110–300ms keyframes, and the nav also mounts with blur/y motion.

Why it mattersNone of these effects is inherently wrong, but together they feel like separate components brought together rather than one control system. The most noticeable inconsistency is that utility controls and showcase cards receive similarly expressive feedback.

Recommended motionDefine three tiers: navigation/utility = color + .98 press only; disclosure = 180–240ms content transition; showcase cards = pointer depth only. Remove lift from secondary links and standardize active scale.

Primary action

Opportunities

  • Rename repeated “View case study” labels to project-specific actions such as “Nidarr process,” “Election flow,” and “Analytics methods” to improve information scent without adding visual noise.
  • Build a small, explicit type-token ladder for display, body, mono metadata, and control labels. This will prevent 7–10px labels from proliferating as new glass objects are added.

Through Jhey’s lens: keep the orbit, pixel field, and evidence carousel as the portfolio’s expressive vocabulary; reduce decoration on ordinary controls so those signature moments can breathe.

07 · Evidence & limitations

The current automated interaction regression passes, so the critical findings are not claims of a broken keyboard implementation. They are based on runtime geometry, touch-target measurements, computed typography values, static CSS review, and desktop/mobile captures. The audit did not modify application code.
